Azurite option sheet

What azurite looks like today, and 14 places it could change. Every picture is the built docs site under data-zao-seal="azurite"; the options are study CSS laid over the real parts, so nothing in the source has changed yet. Pick per gate, or say “leans” for any you want to leave to the lean.

Azurite today

Captured on every component page, every Charts page and the landing page, in light and dark.

Where it appearsPrimary Button (with Composer’s Send and ApprovalGate’s approve), the Switch’s on channel, the primary Tabs line and fishtails, ApprovalGate’s impression and seal well, the docs nav’s current-page mark, and text selection.
Where it is absentMenu, Select, Combobox, TextField, Dialog, Table, Plan, Progress, Card, Conversation, secondary Tabs and all seven charts. Ten of sixteen component pages show azurite only in the nav.
Where it reads weakChroma 0.10 reads as slate; on 2px marks it is close to ink. The dark face is a different, lighter blue. The subtle, border and text roles exist but only the Switch’s edge uses one.
What already holdsLabel contrast is 8.5:1 in light and 7.3:1 in dark; the face stands 8:1 off both grounds; planes step toward black from the seal face in both modes; focus is ink.
  • One fix, not a gate. On the ApprovalGate page the “Ink seal record” specimens turn azurite when the page’s seal is azurite, so both columns look the same. They will be pinned to ink.
  • Not proposed. Status colors, Card, Dialog, Message, the Composer tray, chart references and loading drawings stay as they are in every option.

Which azurite?
