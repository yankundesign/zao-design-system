# Living folio

**Status:** CSS candidate for Su. No published tokens or components change.

## Idea

Bring the close observation of a scientific folio and the _Yingzao Fashi_ diagrams into a useful interface. Warm paper, open writing lines, and section rules make space for content. Swiss alignment and Rams's restraint keep routine actions clear.

## Component decisions

- **Button:** Square corners in every size. Primary is an ink block, secondary a quiet keyline, and quiet gains an underline on hover. Selection is visible without bounce or lift.
- **TextField:** A bottom rule makes the value feel written on the page. Hover strengthens the line; keyboard focus keeps the full outside outline. Error uses the semantic danger line and correction text.
- **Card:** Top and bottom rules form a record without a floating tile. A subtle contour pattern is confined to the data figure, behind a solid label.

## Shared Su constraints

Geist remains the text and display face, including display and title roles. Geist Mono is used only for the record ID. Type roles, spacing, and control heights are unchanged. Surfaces are opaque; motion is a restrained 140 ms line and color response, removed for reduced motion. Light and dark trial palettes retain the same visual grammar.

## What to judge

This has the warmest editorial voice. Check the open field treatment in dense forms and whether the contour figure still serves the data. The study styles the current Button, TextField, and Card hooks. A future Menu should resemble a small inserted sheet with rules, not a blurred floating layer.
