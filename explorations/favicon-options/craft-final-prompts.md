## Option 1: Carved well

Use case: logo-brand.

Crisp deliberate vector-like linework, restrained flat gray return planes, broad light front planes and useful negative space. Strong outer contour and only a few internal construction edges. The outer ink contour should remain visible at 32px (roughly 1/32 of the full canvas width), while internal edges are subordinate. Quiet craft, neither cute cartoon nor glossy 3D clipart.

Precisely closed shapes, constant thickness, correct shared contact, no visibly floating parts. Slightly worked corners, predominantly square geometry, only details that explain construction. The mark fills roughly three quarters of the canvas, with balanced margins. Important silhouette must remain distinct at 16-32px.
Use only flat near-black ink, one neutral middle gray and pale paper. NO soft shading, gradients, cast shadow, separated shadow copies, shine, texture, realistic lighting, detached slabs, exploded diagrams, uneven stroke joins, contradictory perspective, gratuitous bevels, decorative frame around the canvas, labels, mockups, stamps, architectural ornament, watermark. Subject: CARVED SQUARE WELL. ONE continuous squat square block with a broad unbroken rim and one generous square recessed basin carved into its top. The floor is recessed, not a hole through the block. Draw the rim as a coherent closed loop; show just ONE crisp near-side inner wall to establish depth. The top plane is paper-white, the front bearing face is solid near-black, and the narrow right return is flat mid-gray. The basin floor stays pale. The block is nearly front-on with a shallow view into the top, visually compact and almost square overall rather than a wide tray. All wall/rim thicknesses match. No removable lid, floating inner square, repeated nested outlines or separate border frame. It should feel like a small carefully cut material sample with a clear square recess, not a sink with fittings.
