The primary Tabs selection line leads. When the selection changes, the line's leading end travels on the Slide role and its trailing end on Reveal, so it stretches toward the new tab and closes behind it, instead of sliding as one block. Nothing overshoots, the line rests exactly where it did, and reduced motion stays instant. It works in both directions, in RTL and in vertical lists. The indicator is now placed by its four insets, not by a transform and a size; secondary Tabs are unchanged.
